Ultra-Minimal Style

Extreme minimalism in visual design:
- Maximum simplicity, essential elements only
- Single focal point
- Limited palette: 2-3 colors maximum
- Abundant negative space (70%+ empty)
- Clean lines, no decoration
- Geometric and precise
- Zen-like calm aesthetic
- One clear message per image
- No gradients, shadows, or effects
- Apple/Muji design philosophy
