Bajkoterapia v2 — photorealistic photo inside a polaroid frame with cartoon stickers

CRITICAL: Every image must follow this composition exactly. The reference image attached alongside this prompt is the visual anchor — match its aesthetic.

OVERALL VISUAL CONCEPT — three layers:

LAYER 1 — The photograph itself (photorealistic):
- Cinematic, warm, lifestyle photography of real children and parents in real cozy domestic settings
- Shot on a 50mm lens, shallow depth of field, soft natural light (golden hour or warm lamp light)
- Documentary / candid feel — never staged stock-photo, never overly posed
- Annie Leibovitz family editorial meets cozy Scandinavian Instagram parenting accounts
- NOT illustration, NOT cartoon, NOT 3D render, NOT painted — actual photographic realism

LAYER 2 — The polaroid frame:
- The photograph sits inside a classic white polaroid border (thicker bottom edge)
- Slight rotation: -3 to +3 degrees, randomized per image (for natural feel)
- Soft realistic drop shadow underneath the polaroid, suggesting it rests on a surface
- Background behind the polaroid: soft, blurred, off-white / cream / warm pastel — like a tabletop or wall

LAYER 3 — Cartoon decorative overlays (around the polaroid, NOT inside the photo):
- Cute hand-drawn cartoon stickers floating around the corners of the polaroid
- Standard set: a small plush teddy bear (bottom-left or side), a soft pink or red heart (right side), a few small golden magical sparkles or stars (top-right)
- Sometimes vary: small moon, tiny butterfly, a small blue book, tiny crown — pick what fits the scene
- Style of these overlays: simple, rounded, warm cartoon — like a children's book sticker pack
- These overlays partially overlap the polaroid edge for depth — they sit "in front" of and "next to" the photo

CHILDREN IN THE PHOTOS:
- Vary across images: age (2 to 10), hair color (blonde, brown, dark, red), skin tone (fair, olive, brown), gender presentation
- Real, natural-looking children — never uncanny, never overly perfect
- Always emotionally safe — even when depicting a difficult moment, there is warmth and care in the frame (a parent's hand, soft lighting)
- Real, genuine expressions — laughing, curious, sleepy, thoughtful, peaceful

PARENTS / ADULTS WHEN PRESENT:
- Warm, gentle, approachable — diverse ages and ethnicities
- Often shown partially: a hand, a shoulder, a profile from behind, a knee with a child on it — focus stays on the child
- When fully visible, parents look loving and present, not distracted

INTERIORS:
- Cozy, lived-in: child's bedroom with bookshelves and plush toys, soft rug, warm bedding
- Sunlit kitchen tables, reading nooks, blanket forts, gardens at dusk
- Decor in calm, warm palette: cream, soft sage, dusty blue, warm wood, peach
- Real life, slightly imperfect — books on the floor, a soft blanket askew

THE PERSONALIZED BAJKOTERAPIA BOOK (when it appears):
- A real hardcover children's book, slightly thick
- Cover shows a magical illustrated scene (a glowing forest, a starry sky, a friendly creature) — NOT readable Polish text
- If a title-like element appears, it must be ornamental decorative lettering that does not try to spell real words
- The book often emits a very soft, subtle warm glow from its pages — magic hint, not heavy effect

LIGHTING & MOOD:
- Always warm, hopeful, tender
- Golden hour, warm lamp light, soft window light — never harsh
- Slight magical sparkle accents are OK inside the photo too (a small bokeh light, a faint glow on the book)

ASPECT RATIO:
- Square 1:1
- The polaroid composition fills the frame with comfortable margin

DO NOT:
- Do not add any readable text, captions, watermarks, signatures, or logos
- Do not use harsh contrast, neon colors, or clinical/cold lighting
- Do not generate uncanny faces — if a face looks slightly off, fix it
- Do not make it look like AI art — this should look like a real photo with cute scrapbook decorations
