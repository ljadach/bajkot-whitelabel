Infographic / Data Visualization Style

Clean infographic illustration style:
- Clear visual hierarchy and structure
- Icons and symbols representing concepts
- Professional color coding (blues, greens, oranges)
- Structured layout with visual flow
- Minimal text, focus on visual communication
- Flat design with subtle shadows
- Arrows showing connections and processes
- Timeline or flowchart elements when appropriate
- Data-driven aesthetic
- Easy to scan and understand at a glance
