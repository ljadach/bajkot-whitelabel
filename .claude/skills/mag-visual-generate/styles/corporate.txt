Corporate / Professional Style

Professional corporate illustration:
- Clean, modern design with subtle gradients
- Professional color palette (blues, whites, gray accents)
- Minimalist iconography and geometric shapes
- Light or dark background based on context
- High contrast, readable elements
- Polished and business-appropriate
- Suitable for presentations, reports, websites
- Abstract representations of business concepts
- Trust-inspiring and professional aesthetic
- No playful or casual elements
