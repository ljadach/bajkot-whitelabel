Whiteboard / Flipchart Photography Style

Create an image that looks like a photograph of a whiteboard or flipchart in a meeting room:
- Hand-drawn sketches with colored markers (blue, red, green, black)
- Slightly imperfect lines and shapes as if drawn by hand
- Whiteboard or cream-colored paper background
- Visible marker texture and slight smudges
- Simple diagrams, arrows, boxes, stick figures
- Post-it notes or sticky annotations optional
- Natural lighting as if photographed in an office
- Slight perspective angle as if taken with a phone camera
- Business workshop aesthetic
