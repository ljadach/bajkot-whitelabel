Retro / Vintage Style

1960s-70s retro graphic design aesthetic:
- Bold geometric shapes
- Limited color palette with warm tones (orange, brown, mustard, teal)
- Halftone dots and print textures
- Rounded fonts and shapes
- Psychedelic or mod influences
- Grain and noise for aged effect
- Mid-century modern design elements
- Nostalgic and warm feeling
- Poster or album cover aesthetic
- Analog and handcrafted look
