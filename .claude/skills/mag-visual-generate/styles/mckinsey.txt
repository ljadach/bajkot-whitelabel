McKinsey Quarterly / Business Journal Style

Professional business publication illustration style:
- Clean, sophisticated vector-like graphics
- Limited color palette: navy blue, teal, coral/orange accents on white
- Geometric shapes and abstract representations
- Conceptual rather than literal imagery
- High-end editorial aesthetic
- Minimalist with strategic use of negative space
- Subtle gradients and shadows for depth
- No photography - pure illustration
- Evokes strategy, consulting, executive thinking
- Suitable for cover of business magazine or annual report
